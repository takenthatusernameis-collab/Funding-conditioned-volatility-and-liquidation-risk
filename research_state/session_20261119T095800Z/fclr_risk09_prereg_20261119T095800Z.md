## 1. Campaign and family

This is the RISK-09 specification scan for the joint funding × realized-volatility × open-interest interaction family on the 12-month Track A panel. The family has exactly 6 members and is not slimmed:

1. `funding_x_rv = funding_lag * rv_lag`
2. `funding_x_oi = funding_lag * oi_change_lag`
3. `rv_x_oi = rv_lag * oi_change_lag`
4. `three_way = funding_lag * rv_lag * oi_change_lag`
5. `funding_spline` = piecewise-linear monotone spline on `funding_lag` (decile bins at deciles 3/6/9; stdlib only, no scipy)
6. `decile_regime` = indicator(`funding_lag` in top/bottom decile) × indicator(`rv_lag` in top decile)

## 3. Outcome and features (frozen derivations)

- Outcome: `y = 1 if liq_count >= 1 else 0` per (coin, hour). A zero means "no event observed in the feed", not a confirmed no-liquidation hour — this caveat is repeated in every outcome artifact.
- `ret_t = ln(close_t / close_{t-1})` within coin, ordered by `hour_utc`.
- `rv_lag` = sample std (`statistics.stdev`) of trailing 24 hourly log returns ending at `t-1` (`ret_{t-24..t-1}`).
- `funding_lag` = `funding_rate` at `t-1`; `premium_lag` = `premium` at `t-1`.
- `oi_change_lag` = `ln(open_interest_usd_{t-1} / open_interest_usd_{t-2})`; null if `t-2` missing.

## 4. Common-sample rule (binding)

Drop a coin-hour for *all* models whenever any of `rv_lag`, `funding_lag`, `oi_change_lag` is null. All family members and RV-only are scored on the identical test rows. This prevents a fake paired-difference win.

## 5. Models

`intercept-only`, `RV-only` (`rv_lag`), and each of the 6 family members added to the RV-only baseline. Headline contrast: each family member vs RV-only on Brier score. Logistic regression fit by stdlib IRLS; nothing standardized ad hoc.

## 6. Metrics

Brier (headline), log-loss, AUC, calibration OLS slope/intercept on test probabilities.

## 7. Placebo

Permute test labels within hour across coins (label-hour permutation), 1000 permutations, fixed seed `20261119`. Report empirical p and 95th percentile of the null Brier difference per member.

## 8. Multiplicity

Holm over family = `{member_1..member_6} × {scored blocks}` at `alpha=0.05`. Holm family declared in this prereg before outcomes.

## 9. Verdict rule

Per member: `SUPPORT` iff block_3 Brier improvement > 0, `p_raw < 0.05`, `p_holm < 0.05`, and placebo 95th percentile < observed improvement. Otherwise `NOT_MET`. Overall: `SUPPORT` if any member passes; `NOT_MET` if all powered members fail; `UNDERPOWERED` if the power gate fails for all.
