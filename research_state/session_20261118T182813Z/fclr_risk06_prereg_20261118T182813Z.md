## Research question

Do funding-conditioned features improve **out-of-sample probabilistic calibration** of **modeled
liquidation-boundary breach risk** over an RV-only baseline across multiple chronological
holdouts, and is any effect distinguishable from a **funding-permutation placebo**?

## Outcome definition (must never be called observed liquidations)

`synthetic_modeled_boundary_breach`, `observed_liquidation_labels=false`.

- Instruments: `BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, XRPUSDT, BNBUSDT`
- Label months: `2024-04 … 2024-09` (feature history from `2024-03` for trailing 168h windows)
- Entry: close of 1h mark bar `t` (known at `t`); evaluation: hourly mark path `t+1..t+H`
- `mmr=0.005`; `L ∈ {5,10,20}`; `side ∈ {long,short}`; `H ∈ {4,24}`
- Long boundary `P0*(1−1/L)/(1−mmr)`; short boundary `P0*(1+1/L)/(1+mmr)`
- No funding carry, no fees, isolated-margin approximation

## Features (causal at `t`, train-only standardization)

- RV block: `lrv1, lrv24, lrv168`
- Funding block: `fund, fund_abs, fmean, fewma`
- Funding-shock block: `fund_shock = |fund − fewma|`, `fund_z = (fund − fmean)/trailing_sd`

## Models (stdlib IRLS logistic, ridge λ=1e-6, max 100 iters, tol 1e-8, seed 12345)

- `B0_rv_only` = RV block
- `B1_funding_conditioned` = RV + funding block
- `B2_funding_shock` = RV + funding-shock block

## Metrics

- **Primary:** Brier score (lower is better).
- **Paired:** `ΔBrier = Brier_B0 − Brier_Bm` (**positive = funding model better calibrated**).
- **Secondary:** log-loss, AUC.
- Report the pre-registered primary scenario `L10|long|H24` and the equal-weight pooled result over all 12 scenarios.

## Uncertainty and placebo

- UTC-day block bootstrap, seed 12345, **2000 reps**, percentile CI95; LOIO per holdout.
- Placebo: deterministic permutation of the funding series across entry timestamps (seed 12345), identical pipeline.

## Materiality floor (correct sign)

`B1` **or** `B2` beats `B0` (`ΔBrier > 0` with bootstrap CI95 lower bound > 0) in **≥2 of 3**
holdouts on the primary scenario **and** real `|mean ΔBrier|` exceeds placebo `|mean ΔBrier|`.
Otherwise report an honest negative result. Negative results are valid completion; skipping the run is not.
