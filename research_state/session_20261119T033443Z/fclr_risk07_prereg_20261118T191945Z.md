## Question
RISK-06 showed the RV-only baseline (`B0_rv_only`) is the best available score but produced a
compressed predicted-risk range (≈0.6–8%) against observed rates 0–11%. Is that baseline
**reliably miscalibrated out-of-sample**, and can a **train-only recalibration layer** materially
improve **Brier + ECE** across three chronological holdouts and **transfer across scenarios**, beyond
a label-permutation placebo?

## Inputs
- Reused RISK-06 modeled label matrix (`fclr_risk06_labels_20261118T182813Z.json`, 262,080 rows).
- Rebuilt features only from cached 1h mark/funding files, `2024-03 … 2024-09`.
- `mmr=0.005`, `L∈{5,10,20}`, `side∈{long,short}`, `H∈{4,24}` → 12 scenarios, 5 instruments.
- Sealed months `2024-10 …` are never read.

## Base score
`B0_rv_only` = stdlib IRLS logistic on `lrv1,lrv24,lrv168`, ridge `1e-6`, train-only standardization,
one model per scenario pooling all 5 instruments, seed `12345`.

## Calibrators (train-only, fitted on out-of-fold scores)
- `C0_identity` — uncalibrated reference; must reproduce RISK-06 test Brier.
- `C1_platt` — `sigmoid(a·logit(s)+b)` by IRLS with ridge `1e-6`.
- `C2_isotonic` — PAVA on out-of-fold `(s,y)` with tie pooling, monotone step map, linear interpolation, clipped to `[0,1]`.
- `C3_prior_offset` — `sigmoid(logit(s)+logit(p_train)−logit(mean_train_score))`.

## Leakage control (mandatory)
Every calibrator is fitted on **out-of-fold** predictions from **5 contiguous UTC-day-block folds**
inside the train window (each fold model trained on the other four; no cross-day shuffling). The final
base model is refit on the full train window for test scores. Calibrators are never fitted on test rows
or on in-sample train predictions.

## Metrics
- **Brier (primary)**; `ΔBrier = Brier_C0 − Brier_Cm` (positive = calibrated better).
- **ECE** (10 equal-count bins; bins with `<30` rows merged; bin count reported), `ΔECE = ECE_C0 − ECE_Cm`.
- log-loss, AUC, calibration-in-the-large, reliability slope/intercept (diagnostic only).
- Primary scenario `L10|long|H24`; pooled = equal-weight mean over the 12 scenarios.

## Uncertainty
UTC-day block bootstrap, percentile CI95, seed `12345`: `2000` reps for ΔBrier (per-day sums) and
`400` exact-recompute reps for ΔECE. LOIO over instruments on the primary scenario.

## Transfer (secondary, pre-registered)
Fit C1/C3 on scenario A train OOF scores, apply to scenario B test scores; report mean ΔBrier over
ordered pairs `A≠B` and whether the sign agrees with own-scenario calibration.

## Placebo
Permute train labels within scenario **after** OOF scores exist, refit each calibrator on permuted
train scores, apply to the real test set; `200` permutations, seed `12345`. Report null mean and 95th
percentile of mean ΔBrier. The real effect must exceed that 95th percentile.

## Materiality floor (all three required)
1. `C1` or `C3` has `ΔBrier > 0` with bootstrap CI95 lower `> 0` in **≥2 of 3** holdouts on `L10|long|H24`.
2. Mean `ΔECE ≥ 0.005` in **≥2 of 3** holdouts (min-bin guard satisfied).
3. Real mean ΔBrier exceeds the **95th percentile** of the permutation placebo null.

Otherwise the result is an **honest negative**. Skipping the run is not permitted.
